## Private data

Free-tier endpoints may use inputs to improve their models, so no private family details (real
names, places, health, addresses) are sent to Gemini or Cloudflare. The storyboard contains only
the general, widely shared story of the coffee ceremony.

## Cultural representation

The audience is Ethiopian, so the images name specific Ethiopian objects, dress and places
(EXPERIMENTS E12) rather than a generic "African" look. The traditional cross tattoo is kept small
and faint, as worn by many older Orthodox Amhara women, not exaggerated. Ethiopian viewers in the
user tests are asked to flag anything inaccurate.

## Style

The look is described by its qualities ("warm hand-painted gouache illustration, soft golden
light, gentle film grain, earthy palette") and never by naming a living artist.

## Labelling plan

1. **In the film:** a 3-second end card reads "Made with AI" and lists every model used.
2. **On the platform:** turn on the AI-generated / altered content disclosure when posting
   (YouTube "altered or synthetic content", TikTok "AI-generated" label, Meta "AI info").
3. **In the post text:** say plainly that pictures, voice and music were generated by AI from a
   storyboard I wrote.
4. **Provenance:** no watermark or metadata stripping; SynthID in the Gemini audio is left intact.
