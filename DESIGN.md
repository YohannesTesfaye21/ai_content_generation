# Design

## Brief (written before generating anything)

- **Who watches:** Ethiopian young adults, especially in the diaspora (Addis → Dubai, Washington
  DC, Frankfurt), and their parents, who share short videos in family WhatsApp groups and on
  TikTok/Instagram.
- **Where:** on a phone, vertical, often with sound low and subtitles on.
- **What they should feel at the end:** a warm ache of recognition — *"that was my grandmother's
  house too"* — and the urge to call home or make coffee the slow way.
- **What they should understand:** the three cups (abol, tona, baraka) are not just coffee: the
  ceremony is how the family stays together, and anyone can keep it going.
- **How might we:** help scattered Ethiopian families feel close to the grandmother's coffee
  ceremony in 90 seconds?
- **Constraints:** 60–90 s; Amharic narration with English subtitles; free models only; no real
  person's likeness without consent (so the grandmother is a composite character).

## Structure

Three-cup structure as the spine: dawn → roast → smoke → (mortar, cut) → abol → tona → baraka →
the empty room → the grandchild's own cup. The motif is **the third cup**: introduced in s07 as
her rule, missing in s08 (three empty cups), and kept in s09.

## Why each shot exists

| Shot | Size | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| s01 | extreme wide | Establishing: a highland village at dawn, smoke from the roof, before we meet anyone. |
| s02 | close-up (hands) | The ritual is physical and slow; hands carry feeling without showing a face yet. |
| s03 | medium | Introduces her and the gesture of sharing the smoke with everyone. |
| ~~s04~~ | insert | Mortar and pestle, link to her own mother. **Cut** to fit 90 s (EXPERIMENTS E7). |
| s05 | medium close-up | First cup, abol: the pour is the most iconic image of the ceremony. |
| s06 | wide | Second cup, tona: the ceremony is about people, so the family is in frame. |
| s07 | close-up (face) | Emotional peak: the blessing and her rule, nobody leaves before the third cup. |
| s08 | wide, empty | The turn: the same room, dim and empty. Absence shows the scattered family. |
| s09 | medium close-up | Resolution: the grandchild keeps the ritual in a city apartment. |

Shot sizes vary wide → close → medium → close → wide → close on purpose. Experiment E3 shows the
reference image had collapsed this variety into identical portraits until it was fixed.
